Browser limitations

  • Keep this page open and the device awake. Browser tabs can be suspended and recording stops if the tab closes, the browser is killed, or the device sleeps.
  • Microphone access requires permission and usually HTTPS or localhost.
  • Continuous upload consumes Wi-Fi/data and Firebase storage. Stop it when not needed.
  • Audio is saved in the browser's supported recording format (often WebM/Opus), Base64-encoded in Realtime Database.
  • Recording audio does not itself identify pests. A classification model or existing analysis service is needed for pest results.
